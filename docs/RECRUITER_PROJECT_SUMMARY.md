# Recruiter Project Summary

## Project

**Customer 360 & Revenue Growth Analytics**

An end-to-end commercial analytics project combining SQL, Python, statistical modelling and Microsoft Power BI.

## Business Questions Addressed

- How many customers convert and make repeat purchases?
- Which acquisition channels contribute the most revenue?
- Which products perform most strongly?
- How do customer cohorts and RFM segments behave?
- How does conversion vary by device?
- What does short-term revenue forecasting indicate?
- How can experimentation and campaign-efficiency metrics support growth decisions?

## Key Results

- 270,154 customers analysed
- 4,419 purchasing customers
- 775 repeat purchasing customers
- GA4 revenue of 362,165
- Highest-revenue month: December 2020 at 160,555
- Highest-revenue acquisition channel: google / organic at 95,775
- Top product: Google Zip Hoodie F/C at 13,692 revenue
- Revenue forecast holdout MAE: 1,809.72
- Revenue forecast holdout RMSE: 2,278.03

## Modelling

The advanced workflow includes Holt damped-trend revenue forecasting, a 14-day forecast horizon, holdout MAE/RMSE evaluation, reproducible synthetic A/B testing, two-proportion z-testing, and synthetic CPA/ROAS analysis.

## Data Disclosure

Customer, product, acquisition, revenue and forecasting analysis use Google GA4 public sample e-commerce data.

A/B experiment observations are synthetic.

Marketing spend, impressions, clicks and campaign economics are synthetic and are explicitly labelled as such.

## Skills Demonstrated

SQL, Python, pandas, NumPy, SciPy, statsmodels, Power BI, DAX, Power Query, PBIP, PBIR, TMDL, customer analytics, cohort analysis, RFM segmentation, acquisition analysis, product analytics, forecasting, experimentation, marketing analytics and Git.
